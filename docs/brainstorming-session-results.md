## Executive Summary

**Topic:** A simple, sensible todolist application.

**Session Goals:** Focused ideation on core features for a "sensible" to-do list that can be developed in one day, providing comfort and a sense of accomplishment.

**Techniques Used:** 5 Whys

**Total Ideas Generated:** (in progress)

### Key Themes Identified:
- The primary goal is not just task management, but providing emotional comfort and psychological stability.
- The target user feels lonely at work and wants to derive a sense of accomplishment from their tasks.
- The core experience centers on receiving a "little bit of comfort" upon task completion.
- The project has a strong motivation to help others feel more comfortable during their long work hours.

---

## Technique Sessions

### 5 Whys - 
**Description:** A technique to explore the root cause or core value of a problem by asking "Why?" five times.

**Insights Discovered:**
- **1. Why a simple to-do list?** To create one with *sensibility* (감성).
- **2. Why one with *sensibility*?** To get comfort from work due to loneliness.
- **3. Why can a to-do list provide comfort?** By providing a little bit of comfort upon task completion.
- **4. Why is that comfort important?** To feel a sense of accomplishment and psychological stability.
- **5. Why this approach?** Because work is the longest part of the day, and the user wants to find comfort there and help others feel comfortable too.
